The story so far

You're a marine, one of earth's toughest, 
trained for action and hardened in combat. 
three years ago, you assaulted a superior 
officer for ordering his men to fire on 
civilians. he and his body cast were shipped to 
pearl harbor, while you were shipped off to 
mars. mars, home of the union aerospace corporation.
^
The uac is a multi-planetary conglomerate with 
radioactive waste facilities on mars and its 
two moons, phobos and deimos. with no action 
for fifty million miles, your day consisted of 
sucking dust and watching restricted flicks in 
the rec room.
^
The military, uac's biggest customer, has been 
using the moons' facilities to conduct research
into inter-dimensional space travel.
so far they've been able to open gateways between 
phobos and deimos, throwing gadgets into one
and watching them come out the other. 
recently, however, the gateways have grown 
dangerously unstable.
^
"Volunteers" entering them have either 
disappeared or been stricken with a strange 
form of insanity---babbling vulgarities, 
bludgeoning anything that breathes, before 
finally suffering an untimely death: explosive 
dismemberment. matching heads with torsos to 
send home to the folks became a full time job. 
latest reports state that the research 
is encountering a "small setback," but that
everything is under control.
^
A few hours ago, mars received a garbled 
message from phobos. "we need immediate 
support. something fraggin' evil is 
coming out of the gateways! computer systems 
have gone haywire!" the rest was incoherent. 
then deimos simply vanished from the sky. 
attempts to contact both moons have failed.
^
Your squad were sent up pronto to 
phobos. you were to secure the 
perimeter of the base while the rest of your 
team went inside. comms relayed guns firing, 
sergeants yelling orders, screams, bones 
cracking... then finally, silence.
^
It's up to you

Things aren't looking too good. you're 
stranded, and there's no easy way off this 
godforsaken rock. the assault team emptied the 
armory, leaving you with nothing but a pistol 
and a pot to piss in. if only you could lay 
hands on a plasma rifle or even a shotgun,
you could take down a few hostiles on 
the way out. whatever butchered your squad 
deserves a couple of pellets in the head.
^
You check your helmet and exit the dropship.
as you step into the base, animal growls 
echo through distant corridors. they know 
you're here. there's no turning back now.
